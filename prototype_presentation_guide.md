# e-Kapitan: Prototype Presentation Guide (Proposal Defense)

The best way to present a system with multiple user types to a thesis panel is through a **"Scenario-Driven"** approach. Instead of randomly clicking through buttons, you should tell a story that connects all three users: the **Citizen**, the **Staff**, and the **Official**.

Here is a step-by-step script and flow for your live demonstration.

---

## Step 1: The Introduction (1-2 minutes)
**Goal:** Set the stage and explain *how* you will demonstrate the system.

1. **System Overview:** 
   * *"Respected panelists, we present e-Kapitan, our Citizen Service & Digital Local Governance Portal."*
2. **Explain the Interface:** 
   * Point out the **Header** and the **Role Switcher** at the top. 
   * *"To effectively demonstrate the system's capabilities, we have built a Role Switcher into our prototype. This allows us to show you the exact views of our three target users without needing to constantly log in and out during this presentation."*

---

## Step 2: The Citizen Journey (3-4 minutes)
**Goal:** Show how the system makes life easier for the residents. Start here because citizens are the ones who initiate data (requests, complaints).

1. **Switch Role to:** `Resident / Citizen`
2. **Dashboard Overview:** Briefly show the clean, accessible interface. Emphasize the "Large Text" accessibility toggle if your panel cares about senior citizen usability.
3. **Core Action - Document Request:**
   * Go to **Services & Clearances** or the **Document Request Wizard**.
   * Act out a scenario: *"Let's say a resident needs a Barangay Clearance for a job application. They simply go here, fill out the required fields, and submit."*
   * Show the **My Applications** tracker to prove that citizens are no longer left in the dark about their document status.
4. **Highlight Innovation:** Briefly show the **Ka-Barangay AI** to demonstrate that your system uses modern technology to assist users 24/7.

---

## Step 3: The Barangay Staff Journey (3-4 minutes)
**Goal:** Show how the system optimizes the manual, everyday workload inside the barangay hall.

1. **Switch Role to:** `Barangay Staff`
2. **Operations Desk:** Show the Staff Dashboard. Point out how the interface changes completely to focus on operations.
3. **Core Action - Processing the Request:**
   * Go to the **Document Queue**. 
   * *"As you can see, the clearance request we just submitted as a citizen immediately reflects here on the staff's queue."*
   * Show how a staff member would review and approve the document. 
4. **Other Features (Pick One):** 
   * Show the **Smart Counter Queue** (if addressing long lines was in your Chapter 1).
   * OR show the **Incident Blotter Desk** to demonstrate how records are digitized.

---

## Step 4: The Official / Admin Journey (2-3 minutes)
**Goal:** Prove to the panel that the system helps the Barangay Captain and councilors make data-driven decisions.

1. **Switch Role to:** `Officials / Admin`
2. **Executive Intelligence:** Show the Executive Dashboard.
   * *"While the staff handles day-to-day operations, the Barangay Captain needs a bird's-eye view. This dashboard provides analytics on the most requested documents, ongoing complaints, and revenue."*
3. **Transparency & Security:**
   * Show the **Audit Trail**. 
   * *"For security and accountability, officials can see an audit trail of which staff member processed which document."*
4. **QR Verification:**
   * Mention or demonstrate the **Verify QR Code** feature. *"To prevent fake barangay clearances, officials and external agencies can verify printed documents via our QR system."*

---

## Step 5: The Conclusion (1 minute)
**Goal:** Tie the demo back to your Chapter 1 Objectives.

* *"To conclude our demonstration, this prototype validates our Chapter 1 objectives: it streamlines citizen requests, digitizes barangay hall operations, and provides transparency for local officials. This concludes our system walkthrough, we are now ready for your questions."*

---

### 💡 Pro-Tips for the Defense
* **Don't panic about bugs:** If a button doesn't work during the demo, confidently say: *"As this is currently our proposal prototype, that specific backend function is slated for our development phase in Chapter 4. However, the UI flow dictates that it will lead to [explain what it should do]."*
* **Assign roles to your members:** Have one person speaking (the presenter) and another person purely focused on clicking and navigating the prototype smoothly. Practice this coordination!
